Privacy policy

Cenzio is a personal expense tracker. This policy describes the current iOS app and widget.

Effective October 21, 2026

Information you enter

Cenzio stores expenses, categories, budgets, spending goals, monthly income, recurring expenses, archived expenses, learned suggestions, and app preferences that you enter or create. This information is stored on your device. The app and widget share the information needed by the widget through Apple's App Group storage.

Local expense tracking does not require an account. Connected sync is optional and off by default; it uploads records only after separate consent. Cenzio does not sync records through CloudKit. Depending on your device settings, Apple's device backup may include app data; those backups are managed by Apple and your device settings, not by Cenzio.

Optional voice and AI drafts

Recording starts only after you hold and pull the + button, or tap a microphone, and grant permission. Audio is kept in a temporary protected file on this iPhone, transcribed locally after Stop, and deleted after transcription or cancellation. The first use may download an Apple speech model. Recorded audio is not uploaded. Keyboard dictation follows your iPhone's own settings.

The first time you use AI expense preparation, the app asks permission to send recognized text, current category names and IDs, currency, reference date and timezone through the authenticated server to OpenRouter/OpenAI. After you agree, stopping a recording automatically sends this context for an AI draft. Past expenses, income, budgets and audio are not sent. Provider retention policies apply; this is not a zero-retention guarantee. Draft text is kept temporarily in server memory for retry handling, not in its expense database. Nothing is saved until you review and tap Save.

The OpenRouter key stays on your server. The phone's separate credential is stored in iOS Keychain without iCloud Keychain synchronization. Disconnect in Voice connection to remove it and request revocation. If your server is offline, revoke the device on the server separately. Clearing app data also removes the phone's connection.

Optional connected sync

After you enable connected sync and consent, expenses, archived expenses, categories, currency and timezone are sent to Cenzio's configured sync service. Budgets, salary and recorded audio are not included. The service retains changes and older versions for history and restore. A random installation credential is stored in device-only iOS Keychain to keep ledgers separate; this is not a cross-device account or recovery service.

You choose your own Google or Notion account and which Notion pages to share. Provider tokens are encrypted on the service, not bundled in the app. Independently authorized MCP AI clients have their own ledger permissions; Google or Notion enrollment does not authorize an AI client. Connected services have their own privacy policies. Disconnecting does not erase history, backups or external copies. Contact support about retained service copies, and manage external copies and permissions in the connected account.

If you explicitly select an existing Notion table and approve a mapping preview, its expense rows are read by the service to check the chosen columns and categories. This read-only preview does not import rows into your ledger, enable sync or change the existing table.

Imports and exports

CSV files that you choose to import are processed on your device. When you export data, Cenzio creates a file on your device and lets you choose where to save or share it. Once you share or save an exported copy elsewhere, that copy is controlled by the destination you chose.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The current app does not include third-party advertising or analytics, does not sell your data, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Support email

If you choose to email support, your email address and anything you include in the message are sent through your email provider. We use that message only to respond to your request. Please do not include financial details that are not needed to explain the issue.

Retention and deletion

Your local Cenzio records remain on your device until you delete them in Settings or remove the app. Exports, support email, server history, backups and connected-service records are separate copies and are not deleted when you clear app data. Delete external copies and revoke connections separately; contact your server administrator about retained history and backups. If you restore an iOS device backup, app data may be restored with it.
