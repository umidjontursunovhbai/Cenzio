Where are my expenses stored?

In the current version, your Cenzio records are stored on your device. Cenzio does not require an account or sync them to a Cenzio server. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I keep a copy of my data?

Open Cenzio → Settings → Data Management → Export Data. Choose where to save the exported file. Keep that copy somewhere you can access if you change phones or remove the app.

How do I delete my data?

Open Cenzio → Settings → Clear All Data. Exported files you saved elsewhere and support emails you sent are separate copies; remove those from their destinations separately.
